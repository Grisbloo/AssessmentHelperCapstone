### 1.2 Overdue Evaluation Count

Number of faculty members whose required evaluation deadline has passed without the evaluation being completed.

**Calculation**

KPI = Count of faculty with Evaluation Due Date < Current Date AND Evaluation Status != Complete

**Required Data**

* Faculty evaluation due date
* Evaluation completion status
* Last evaluation date
* Faculty hiring level / evaluation cycle


**How to use**

A high overdue count indicates that evaluations are not being scheduled or completed on time. This can be used to identify faculty members requiring immediate attention and determine whether evaluation scheduling needs to begin earlier.

## 2. Assessment Participation KPIs

### 2.1 Assessment Participation Rate

Percentage of faculty expected to participate in the assessment process who actually participate.

**Calculation**

KPI =  Participating Faculty / Faculty Expected to Participate x 100

Participating Faculty = Faculty who complete at least one required action in the assessment process

**Required Data**

* Faculty expected to participate
* Assessment sign-ups
* Observation assignments
* Assessment activity/completion status


**How to use**

A low participation rate may indicate that faculty are not signing up, responding, or completing required assessment actions. This can help determine whether additional reminders, clearer instructions, or changes to the workflow are needed.

### 2.2 Observer Utilization Rate

Percentage of available observers who are assigned at least one observation.

**Calculation**

KPI = Observers Assigned at Least One Observation / Total Available Observers x 100

**Required Data**

* List of eligible/available observers
* Observation assignments
* Observer availability
* Semester
* Faculty focus areas

**How to use**

A low utilization rate may indicate that observations are being concentrated among a small number of observers. A high utilization rate indicates that the available observer pool is being used effectively.

This KPI should be considered together with Average Observations per Observer so that a high utilization rate does not hide individual observers being overloaded.

### 2.3 Average Observations per Observer

Average number of observations assigned to each active observer.

**Calculation**

KPI = Total Number of Assigned Observations / Number of Active Observers

Active Observer = Observer assigned at least one observation

**Required Data**

* Observation assignments
* Observer IDs
* Semester
* Observation status

**How to use**

This KPI can be used to monitor observer workload. A high average may indicate that there are not enough observers, while a very low average may indicate that available observers are being underutilized.

The average should also be compared with individual observer workloads to identify uneven assignment distribution.

## 3. Recommendation Engine KPIs

### 3.1 Focus-Area Match Accuracy

Percentage of observer recommendations that correctly match the faculty member's required focus area.

**Calculation**

KPI = Correct Focus-Area Matches / Total Observer Recommendations x 100

Correct Focus-Area Match = Recommended observer satisfies the required focus-area matching rules

**Required Data**

* Faculty focus areas
* Observer focus areas
* Observer recommendations generated by the system
* Matching rules
* TODO: Define what qualifies as a correct or acceptable focus-area match

**How to use**

A low percentage indicates that the recommendation algorithm needs improvement. The matching logic can be adjusted to prioritize observers with more relevant expertise.

If the percentage is high but there are frequently not enough recommendations, the matching requirements may be too restrictive and should be reviewed together with List Sufficiency Rate.

### 3.2 List Sufficiency Rate

Percentage of faculty evaluations for which the recommendation engine produces enough valid observer choices.

**Calculation**

KPI = Faculty with Sufficient Recommendation Lists / Faculty Requiring Observer Recommendations x 100

Sufficient Recommendation List = Recommendation list containing at least the required number of valid observers

**Required Data**

* Number of recommendations generated for each faculty member
* Required minimum number of observers
* Observer eligibility
* Observer availability
* Focus-area matching results
* TODO: Define the minimum number of observer recommendations considered sufficient

**How to use**

A low sufficiency rate indicates that the system frequently cannot find enough qualified observers. This may mean that the observer pool is too small or that matching requirements are too restrictive.

This KPI should be compared with Focus-Area Match Accuracy. The goal is to provide enough recommendations without lowering the quality of the matches too far.
